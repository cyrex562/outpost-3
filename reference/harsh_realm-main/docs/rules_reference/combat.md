# Combat Rules Reference (XWN)

## Initiative

Roll d8 + DEX modifier. Higher result goes first. Ties broken by DEX score; further ties broken randomly.

## Attack Roll

d20 + attack_bonus + skill (Stab / Shoot / Punch) + attribute_modifier vs target AC.

- Natural 1: always misses regardless of modifiers.
- Natural 20: always hits regardless of target AC.

Attribute modifiers for attack:
- Melee weapons (stab, punch): STR modifier
- Ranged weapons (shoot): DEX modifier

## Damage

Weapon die + STR modifier (melee) or DEX modifier (ranged).

Warriors add half their level (rounded up) as bonus damage.

Minimum damage on a hit is 1.

## Saving Throws

d20 vs 15 - half level (round down). Roll equal to or over the target to succeed.

## Surprise

Determined by awareness check before combat begins:
- Player surprise: player detects enemy, enemy does not detect player. Player may act freely in round 1; enemies skip their turns.
- Enemy surprise: enemy detects player, player does not detect enemy. Enemies act freely in round 1; player skips their turn.
- Mutual awareness: both sides ready; normal initiative.
